    <p data-block-key="w80sg">Today, we are introducing <b>on-device Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)</b> and <b>Text-to-Speech (TTS)</b> capabilities in <a href="https://developers.google.com/edge/gallery">Google AI Edge Gallery</a>. Voice is often the most natural way to interact with AI on phones and wearables. Recent advances in compact speech architectures now make it possible to run accurate, <a href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/hf-audio/open_asr_leaderboard">multilingual ASR</a> and <a href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/hf-audio/open_tts_leaderboard">expressive TTS</a> models entirely on device, all within a small memory footprint, with low latency, and completely offline. By pairing these lightweight speech models with on-device small language models (SLMs) like Gemini Nano and Gemma 4, developers can build apps that stream live transcriptions as the user speaks and synthesize natural spoken replies locally.</p><p data-block-key="3f93k">This release brings together major advancements across both Google’s system speech models and the open-weight ecosystem:</p><p data-block-key="2iggq"></p><ul><li data-block-key="2tg0q"><b>Android: faster, more accurate system speech models with ML Kit:</b> Powered by Google’s latest on-device speech models, ML Kit delivers incredibly accurate multilingual transcription across 100+ languages at 10x real-time speed. It is paired with a few-step diffusion TTS model that begins speaking in just ~100 ms and synthesizes 24 kHz audio at up to 50x real-time speed. Both models are integrated into the OS, adding 0 MB to your APK.</li><li data-block-key="3mi2v"><b>Web, Desktop and Beyond: cross-platform, open-weight speech with MediaPipe Tasks and LiteRT:</b> For cross-platform apps, we optimized new .litertlm model speech bundles, including Parakeet-TDT 0.6B, PocketTTS 100M, Kokoro-82M, and Phonon 31M. They deliver real-time streaming transcription and low-latency speech synthesis in a compact memory footprint, and run smoothly alongside on-device SLMs without compute contention.</li></ul><p data-block-key="6idnm"></p><p data-block-key="3vudq">Below, we’ll explore how this local voice pipeline powers the new <b>XR (Extended Reality) Language Teacher</b> showcase, upgrades <b>AI Chat</b> in Google AI Edge Gallery, and how you can build with these APIs and models today.</p><h2 data-block-key="fistr" id="multimodal-voice-tutoring-with-xr-language-teacher">Multimodal voice tutoring with XR Language Teacher</h2><p data-block-key="5cd1b">Learning a language while traveling happens in the real world, often in underground stations, busy markets, or abroad where Wi-Fi and roaming data may not be available. To show how vision, language, and speech models can work together <b>100% locally on your phone</b> with no network connection needed, we built the <b>XR Language Teacher</b> showcase in Google AI Edge Gallery. Simply point your camera at an unfamiliar object or menu, ask <i>&quot;What is that, and how do I ask for one?&quot;</i>, and immediately hear an explanation in your language alongside authentic pronunciation in the local language.</p>

    <h3 data-block-key="6ea2k" id=""><b>Seeing, speaking, and learning entirely on device</b></h3><ul><li data-block-key="4buvk"><b>Live visual grounding and streaming voice input:</b> Point your camera at a street sign, menu, or object and ask a question aloud. On-device ASR streams your spoken question in real time alongside the camera frame to <b>Gemini Nano</b> on Android devices —or <b>Gemma 4</b> across other platforms—in a single pass.</li><li data-block-key="3mq9s"><b>Structured outputs for bilingual TTS code-switching:</b> Using a structured output schema, the model explains the scene in your own language while tagging foreign-language phrases separately, allowing the TTS pipeline to seamlessly switch voices and accents mid-sentence from your mother tongue to the target language alongside an on-screen vocabulary card.</li></ul><h3 data-block-key="91ycm" id=""><b>Adapt for intelligent eyewear</b></h3><p data-block-key="38ehb">With inference running locally on a paired phone, the same experience works standalone on a phone and extends naturally to hands-free smart glasses. To see how we adapted Language Teacher for intelligent glasses check out our recent talk at <b>Android Dev Summit:</b> <a href="https://youtu.be/tW3mg0iyi_w?si=Qg5lsPUiYCRNg4_y"><i>Building Audio-First Experiences for Intelligent Eyewear with Gemini Nano</i></a><i>.</i></p><h2 data-block-key="34jeh" id="streaming-voice-dictation-and-read-aloud-playback-in-ai-chat">Streaming voice dictation and read-aloud playback in AI Chat</h2><p data-block-key="chok4">Beyond the XR Language Teacher showcase, we also built ASR and TTS directly into Google AI Edge Gallery’s main <b>AI Chat</b> feature, giving on-device SLMs like Gemini Nano and Gemma 4 a natural, offline voice interface while on the go.</p>

    <h3 data-block-key="7icm7" id="sentence-synchronized-playback-and-configurable-speech-settings"><b>Sentence-synchronized playback and configurable speech settings</b></h3><ul><li data-block-key="cm4os"><b>ASR and TTS settings in Model Management:</b> Choose between available ASR and TTS pipelines, preset voices, and downloaded language packs in one central place.</li><li data-block-key="des45"><b>Read-aloud playback with live sentence highlighting:</b> Turn on “Auto-play TTS” or tap the speaker button on any response to launch a floating TTS bar that highlights each spoken sentence in real time, with the ability to jump to any sentence by tapping it.</li><li data-block-key="esceg"><b>Reference implementation for chat voice outputs:</b> Our open-source codebase demonstrates how to strip Markdown syntax, skip non-spoken blocks like code snippets and tables, and schedule synthesis at the sentence level to avoid compute and memory contention during LLM token generation.</li></ul><h2 data-block-key="x5cj8" id="under-the-hood:-building-with-ml-kit-mediapipe-tasks-and-litert">Under the Hood: Building with ML Kit, MediaPipe Tasks, and LiteRT</h2><p data-block-key="ekit2">While multimodal models like Gemma 4 can process audio inputs directly, we provide a modular solution for cascading language models with separate, lightweight voice models for latency optimization and flexibility. Depending on your target devices and model requirements, our <a href="https://github.com/google-ai-edge/gallery">open-source code on GitHub</a> shows you the optimized and recommended solution for Android using <b>ML Kit</b>, as well as <b>MediaPipe Tasks</b> and <b>LiteRT</b> for cross-platform coverage and open model support.</p><h3 data-block-key="gq55b" id="building-for-flagship-android-with-ml-kit"><b>Building for flagship Android with ML Kit</b></h3><p data-block-key="1iakf">For developers building for Android devices, <b>ML Kit’s GenAI Speech Recognition and Text-to-Speech APIs</b> provide a standardized, platform-integrated path out of the box. With the underlying speech models managed through <b>AICore</b> and Google’s on-device speech services, developers get production-grade quality without having to manage model downloads or worry about APK size bloat. Your app also benefits automatically from hardware acceleration across NPU and TPU backends (when available) and seamless background model updates.</p><p data-block-key="ca6rs"></p><ul><li data-block-key="8ilee"><a href="https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/genai/speech-recognition/android"><b>ML Kit GenAI Speech Recognition API</b></a><b>:</b> Streams live partial and final transcriptions as the user speaks, accepting audio from either the built-in device microphone or external audio streams such as smart glasses. Our latest on-device ASR advancements deliver incredibly accurate multilingual recognition across 100+ languages (reaching 8.0% to 9.0% Word Error Rate on FLEURS) while transcribing speech 10x faster than the real time audio being transcribed (~300 ms per 3 seconds of speech) and cutting end-to-end speech translation wait times by 35% to 44%.</li><li data-block-key="ecc4i"><a href="https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/genai/text-to-speech/android"><b>ML Kit GenAI Text-to-Speech API</b></a><b>:</b> Powered by a compact, few-step diffusion model accelerated on NPU, the on-device TTS begins speaking in ~100 ms and generates natural 24 kHz speech across 8 expressive voices and 21 locales at up to 50x real-time speed. It synthesizes a 27-second paragraph in just ~500 ms while cutting active device power by over 50% compared to traditional autoregressive TTS models, and includes built-in earcon support.</li></ul><p data-block-key="8ucub"></p><h3 data-block-key="hd8ab" id="building-across-platforms-with-mediapipe-tasks"><b>Building across platforms with MediaPipe Tasks</b></h3><p data-block-key="bbi3e">Integrating ASR and TTS into cross-platform applications usually requires handling low-level audio framing, Voice Activity Detection (VAD), and complex decoding pipelines. <a href="https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/tasks"><b>MediaPipe Task</b></a> simplifies this by providing unified, turnkey ASR and TTS APIs across iOS, macOS, Windows, Linux, and Web that abstract away manual pre-processing, audio stream routing, and platform-specific audio management into simple, high-level interfaces:</p><p data-block-key="7b9nr"></p><ul><li data-block-key="3qe9q"><a href="https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/audio/audio_transcriber?_gl=1*1hit3fh*_up*MQ..*_ga*NzI2NTUwMTI5LjE3OTMzMzIwNDQ.*_ga_SM8HXJ53K2*czE3OTMzMzIwNDMkbzEkZzAkdDE3OTMzMzIwNDMkajYwJGwwJGgw"><b>MediaPipe Speech Recognition (ASR) Task</b></a><b>:</b> Automatically handles audio framing, configurable Voice Activity Detection (VAD), and decoding. It supports real-time streaming, full-sentence batch, and hybrid transcription modes (streaming live words as the user speaks and refining the full sentence once speech ends), and showcases open-weight models like <b>Parakeet-TDT 0.6B</b> out of the box. <a href="https://google-ai-edge.github.io/mediapipe-samples-web/#/audio/audio_transcriber">Try it now</a> directly in the browser!</li><li data-block-key="6ufif"><a href="https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/audio/text_to_speech?_gl=1*1hit3fh*_up*MQ..*_ga*NzI2NTUwMTI5LjE3OTMzMzIwNDQ.*_ga_SM8HXJ53K2*czE3OTMzMzIwNDMkbzEkZzAkdDE3OTMzMzIwNDMkajYwJGwwJGgw"><b>MediaPipe Text-to-Speech (TTS) Task</b></a><b>:</b> Provides a turnkey synthesis interface with voice selection and low-latency streaming audio output, showcasing open-weight models out of the box, including multilingual models like <b>PocketTTS 100M</b> and <b>Kokoro-82M</b>, as well as ultra-lightweight English models like <b>Phonon 31M</b> with sub-100ms Time-to-First-Audio (TTFA) on most devices and platforms. <a href="https://google-ai-edge.github.io/mediapipe-samples-web/#/audio/text_to_speech">Try it now</a> directly in the browser!</li></ul><p data-block-key="3tuhm"></p><h3 data-block-key="j2qoo" id="powering-cross-platform-speech-and-flexible-model-support-with-litert"><b>Powering cross-platform speech and flexible model support with LiteRT</b></h3><p data-block-key="bgnbt">Under the hood, ML Kit and MediaPipe Tasks are powered by <a href="https://developers.google.com/edge/litert"><b>LiteRT</b></a>, Google’s high-performance on-device AI inference infrastructure. Cross-platform applications requiring even more fine-grained control over speech processing or custom model architectures can also build on it directly.</p><p data-block-key="e2ich">Speech models often rely on multiple subgraphs and auxiliary assets, such as encoder/decoder subgraphs and tokenizers for ASR, or phonemizer dictionaries, voice embeddings, and vocoders for TTS. All required components for each model are packaged into a single .litertlm file for easy deployment. The app ships with ready-to-use .litertlm models, including <a href="https://huggingface.co/litert-community/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3">Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3</a> (multilingual ASR), <a href="https://huggingface.co/litert-community/pocket-tts">PocketTTS 100M</a> (multilingual TTS), <a href="https://huggingface.co/litert-community/phonon-31m-en">Phonon 31M</a> (ultra-lightweight English TTS), and <a href="https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Kokoro-82M">Kokoro-82M</a> (multilingual TTS). Beyond these, the vibrant <a href="https://huggingface.co/litert-community"><b>Hugging Face LiteRT Community</b></a> with over 10K+ followers offers a growing catalog of open-weight models, like Whisper, Qwen3-ASR and Moonshine for ASR, or Qwen3-TTS and Matcha for TTS. For developers seeking custom implementations or support for additional open-weight voice models, LiteRT provides a rich set of <a href="https://github.com/google-ai-edge/LiteRT-LM/tree/main/omni#readme">multimodal model examples</a> to help you get started, with more on the way.</p><h2 data-block-key="s8hqx" id="get-started-today">Get started today</h2><p data-block-key="ble1f">Adding a local voice pipeline takes on-device SLMs beyond typing-based chat interfaces, enabling natural, voice-first interactions that work anywhere, completely offline. All the building blocks to try these experiences or integrate them into your own apps are available today:</p><p data-block-key="f04kl"></p><ul><li data-block-key="9oahj"><b>Experience the new voice features:</b> Download the latest <a href="https://developers.google.com/edge/gallery">Google AI Edge Gallery app</a> on <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.ai.edge.gallery">Android</a> and <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-ai-edge-gallery/id6749645337?itscg=30200&amp;itsct=apps_box_badge&amp;mttnsubad=6749645337">iOS</a> to try <i>XR Language Teacher</i> and voice-enabled <i>AI Chat</i> on your own device.</li><li data-block-key="c678t"><b>Explore and adapt the open-source code:</b> Check out the reference implementation in the <a href="https://github.com/google-ai-edge/gallery">Google AI Edge Gallery GitHub repository</a>, or point your favorite coding agent, such as <a href="https://antigravity.google/">Google Antigravity</a>, at our GitHub repo to quickly adapt the streaming ASR, bilingual TTS code-switching, and sentence-synchronized playback pipeline for your own app.</li><li data-block-key="2qva8"><b>Start building with ML Kit, MediaPipe Tasks, and LiteRT:</b> Explore the <a href="https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/genai/speech-recognition/android">ML Kit GenAI Speech Recognition documentation</a> for Android integrations, <a href="https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions">MediaPipe Tasks</a> for turnkey cross-platform APIs, or follow the <a href="https://developers.google.com/edge/litert">LiteRT developer guide</a> and download ready-to-run .litertlm bundles from the <a href="https://huggingface.co/litert-community">Hugging Face LiteRT Community</a>. Test and benchmark your on-device voice pipelines across 200+ real physical devices in Google Cloud’s <a href="https://cloud.google.com/products/developer-device-platform">Developer Device Platform</a>.</li><li data-block-key="1bds0"><b>Watch the Android Dev Summit session:</b> Check out our <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@AndroidDevelopers">Android Dev Summit session recording</a> to see how Language Teacher is adapted for intelligent eyewear and learn more engineering tips from building the pipeline.</li></ul><p data-block-key="f6cvt"></p><h3 data-block-key="3ipdm" id="acknowledgements-(alphabetical-order)"><b>Acknowledgements</b> <i>(alphabetical order)</i></h3><p data-block-key="f2rl4"><i>We&#x27;d like to extend a special thanks to our</i> <b><i>significant contributors</i></b> <i>for their work on this project:</i></p><p data-block-key="jvf1"><i><sup>Alice Zheng, Ajay Dudani, Alex Kanaukou, Amy Zeppenfeld, Byungchul Kim, Cheng Yang, Chintan Parikh, Chris Parsons, Cormac Brick, David Chou, Dong Chen, Geonsun Lee, Glenn Cameron, Hriday Chhabria, Ian Ballantyne, Ireneu Pla, James O’Donnell, Jamieson Pryor, Jason Poon, Jenn Lee, Jianing Wei, Jing Jin, Jingxiao Zheng, Jingyu Shi, Karthik Thirumalai, Kristen Wright, Lu Wang, Marius Kintel, Matthias Grundmann, Miao Wang, Mogan Shieh, Na Li, Paul Hsia, Qiumin Xu, Ravi Shankar, Rishika Sinha, Ronald Wotzlaw, Sachin Kotwani, Sandeep Patil, Sebastian Schmidt, Shao-Fu Shih, Shuangfeng Li, Suril Shah, Tiger Souvannakoumane, Tzu-Ping Chen, Weiyi Wang, Wentao Tu, Yang Yang, Yi-Chun Kuo, Yishuang Pang, Yu-che Liu, Yu-hui Chen, Yuling Liang, Zhihao Yang, Zichuan Wei.</sup></i></p>
